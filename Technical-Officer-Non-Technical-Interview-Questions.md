# Non-Technical English Interview Questions and Answers for Technical Officer

## 1. Could you please tell us about yourself?

My name is Jialei Xiao. I have more than ten years of work experience in software-related roles. I am a careful and responsible person. I enjoy working with people to solve real problems.

## 2. Why are you interested in this Technical Officer position?

This role matches my work experience and my working style. I enjoy supporting users and helping different teams work better together. I am looking for a role where I can do useful work and grow for a long time.

## 3. Why are you looking for a new job?

I learned a lot in my previous role and gained valuable experience. Now I would like to find a new challenge and a more long-term opportunity. I hope to support a team with my experience and learn more about a new work area.

## 4. What are your main strengths at work?

I am responsible, patient, and careful with details. I try to understand the real need before I start work. I also communicate clearly and follow up on problems until they are solved.

## 5. What is one weakness you are working on?

Sometimes I spend too much time checking small details. I know that time is important, so I now set clear priorities first. I also ask for feedback early when needed.

## 6. How do you work with people from different departments?

I listen carefully to understand their needs and problems. I use simple words and confirm important details. I give clear updates so that everyone knows the progress and next step.

## 7. Can you describe a challenging work situation?

Sometimes a request is urgent but the details are not clear. In this case, I first confirm the most important goal and the possible impact. Then I break the work into small steps and keep the team updated.

## 8. How do you handle several urgent tasks at the same time?

I check which task has the biggest impact and the nearest deadline. I discuss priorities with my manager or the team if needed. Then I focus on the most important task first and give regular updates.

## 9. How do you respond when a user is unhappy with a problem?

First, I listen carefully and stay calm. I try to understand the problem and tell the user what I will do next. After the problem is solved, I follow up to make sure the user is satisfied.

## 10. What do you hope to achieve in this role?

I hope to become a reliable member of the team. I want to understand the organization’s work and help improve daily processes for users. I also hope to learn, grow, and make useful contributions over time.
